Japanese Beef Curry

Japanese beef curry is usually made with a thick curry sauce simmered with tender beef, potatoes, carrots, and onions.

The beef becomes soft and juicy after slow cooking, while the vegetables absorb the delicious curry flavour. It's often served with steamed rice.

Is it easy to make? Yes! It's as easy as finishing a Japanese beef curry rice bowl!

Check Home for more recipes

Ingredients

  • Diced Beef Braising Steak x400g
  • carrot x 1
  • Potatoes x 2
  • Onion x 1
  • Japanese curry mix x 1 package

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients. Cut the vegetables into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Heat oil. Cook beef and onions until browned. Add potatoes and carrots.
  3. Pour in the water and bring to a boil. Skim off any foam. Reduce heat, and simmer for about 30-40 minutes.
  4. Dissolve the curry mix blocks.
  5. Turn the heat on low. Stir occasionally and let it simmer for another 5-10 minutes until the sauce thickens. Adjust flavor with soy sauce, oyster sauce, chilli flakes if desired.
  6. Serve with hot white rice. Enjoy the warm, rich, and comforting Japanese beef curry rice!
