Chinese Pork Rib Soup

There are different kinds of pork rib soup in China, depending on which ingredients you add. This recipe uses winter melon(also called wax gourd, or donggua in Chinese).

If you don't know what winter melon is or can't find it in your local supermarket, feel free to use carrot or potato instead. They work just as well!

Winter melon pork rib soup is a light, nourishing soup that may help cool the body, reduce swelling, support healthy skin, and strengthens bones. Perfect for keeping you refreshed and energized!

Ingredients

Here are the ingredients I usually use to make winter melon pork rib soup:

  • Pork ribs
  • Winter melon (wax gourd, or donggua)
  • Spring onion
  • Chinese cooking wine (shaoxing wine)
  • Coriander

Steps

  1. Blanch the pork ribs

    • Place the ribs in cold water and bring to a boil. Add cooking wine and spring onion to help remove any unpleasant smell.
    • While the ribs are blanching, boil a kettle of hot water.
    • Once the water comes to a boil, drain the ribs and rinse them with the hot water to remove blood and impurities.

  2. Peel and cut the winter melon

    • Remove the seeds and soft pulp, then peel and cut the winter melon into cubes.

  3. Prepare the soup base

    • Place the blanched ribs, winter cubes, and enough water into the inner pot of the pressure cooker. No seasoning is needed at this stage. Close the lid and cook on high pressure for about 25-30 minutes

    A pressure cooker makes the meat tender, juicy, and full of flavour.

  4. Season

    • After cooking, carefully release all pressure before opening the cooker.

    Always ensure the pressure is fully released before opening the lid.

    • Add salt, soy sauce, pepper, chopped coriander and the seasoning packet from the Korean noodle pack.
    • Stir gently to blend the flavours.

  5. Serve

    • Ta-da! Your light,nourishing and flavourful winter melon pork rib soup is ready.· Serve hot and enjoy!
